# Changelog

## 1.6.0 (2026-10-09)

The first public release.

- A big, simple home screen: clock, date and battery; the family as photo tiles; WhatsApp; apps as big icons.
- A tap on a person: WhatsApp, Anrufen and SMS as three big buttons.
- Liquid glass that bends a background photo at the edges, with a fine specular rim; Inter as the typeface.
- Can't be left by accident (Home always comes back, Back does nothing on the front page); settings behind a 4-second
  hold and an optional PIN; the layout can be arranged and locked.
- Updates itself from these releases, quietly while the screen is off (Android 12+, after "Automatische Updates
  einschalten" once).
